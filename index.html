How to use Revision Loop

Keyboard Shortcuts:

  • Space — Flip the flashcard
  • 1 — Again (10 mins)
  • 2 — Hard (1 day)
  • 3 — Good (3 days)
  • 4 — Easy (7 days)

Math & Science Formulas:

Wrap formulas in dollar signs to make them look real. E.g., $H_2O$ becomes H₂O, and $E=mc^2$ becomes E=mc².
